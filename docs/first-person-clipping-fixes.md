# First-person clipping and map selection

The map menu now builds both its native selector and five visible thumbnail cards from the actual `MAPS` catalog, including Sunset. Cards use the same host/public-room permissions and map-change action as the selector; manual solo changes clear an unrelated pre-aim route query. Current selection is exposed with `aria-pressed`, and disabled room controls apply to both inputs.

The world camera keeps its existing 0.1 near plane and normal depth precision. Camera-local weapons, arms, held Spike and ability hands render in a separate layer after clearing depth, with a 0.012 near plane. They keep internal depth testing and original lighting/materials. This prevents close map surfaces, the cosmetic body and the world near plane from cutting gun skins or hands. Local and spectator cameras use the same pass; inactive-camera viewmodels are hidden only during the overlay render. Drone cameras keep their world view. Camera layers/projection, background, clear flags, scene matrix updates, shadow update settings and renderer counters restore even if rendering throws. Shadow maps and world matrices are not rendered/recomputed a second time for the overlay.

The cosmetic body previously kept only triangles whose three vertices had enough leg/pelvis weight, discarding mixed-weight clothing and leaving irregular open surfaces. It now retains source lower-body clothing regardless of whether that clothing is weighted to the spine, removes detached head/arm surfaces and uses a pelvis-following clipping plane. A small dark cap is built from the stand-pose waist intersection to close the opening. Both sides of source cloth render. A 0.9 cosmetic scale aligns the source body's head height with the 2.2 game eye height; the body remains behind the camera. Standing/crouching/walking update the waist plane and cap anchor. This is an authored first-person crop/cap, not an original extracted first-person body mesh. Third-person avatars, hitboxes, collision height and network poses are unchanged.

No gun skins, native rigs, poses, skin weights or firing/economy rules are replaced. Existing asynchronous hand/model disposal and texture ownership remain in use. The camera-local render layer assumes held meshes stay attached to their camera; deployed projectiles/devices continue to use their separate world meshes.

Validation: `tests/test_first_person_clipping_browser.cjs` covers nine new gun skins across both Jett/Phoenix arm families at idle, maximum downward pitch and reload, checks all 28 selectable bodies standing/crouching, verifies intact gun/hand pixels against an opaque near-camera wall, and navigates to Sunset through its card. `VALO_VISUAL_SKINS=blastx-phantom,smite-ghost` limits skin cases while retaining all body cases. `tests/test_first_person_pass.mjs` verifies world/overlay near planes, depth separation and state restoration on success/failure. Existing spectator browser checks pass; source-model test capture helpers now use the actual gameplay render pass.
